Research internship · NCP Islamabad · 2026

LaMnO₃ / NiFe₂O₄ hybrid electrodes for pseudocapacitive energy storage

Research question. Can combining a perovskite oxide (LaMnO₃) with a spinel oxide (NiFe₂O₄) create complementary redox-active sites and interfaces that are useful for electrochemical energy storage?

Project approach. The internship project focused on synthesis and hybrid-electrode design, followed by materials and electrochemical evaluation. The presentation discusses the roles of the two oxide phases, electrode fabrication, structural characterisation and electrochemical testing.

Methods and analysis covered

Cyclic voltammetry (CV), galvanostatic charge–discharge (GCD), electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS), and materials characterisation. The project presentation also reviews charge-storage mechanisms and performance benchmarking.

Undergraduate thesis · 2025–2026

Formation of spinel ferrites (Li₂Fe₂₋ₓInₓO₄) by sol–gel method

Research question. How does partial substitution of iron by indium affect the structural and dielectric characteristics of lithium-based spinel ferrites?

Composition series. The thesis studies Li₂Fe₂₋ₓInₓO₄ with x = 0.0, 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3. The samples were prepared using a sol–gel route and investigated through structural, spectroscopic and impedance-related measurements.

What the analysis considered. X-ray diffraction (XRD) was used to examine phase formation and lattice parameters; FTIR bands were discussed in relation to metal–oxygen vibrations. The thesis also evaluates dielectric properties and impedance behaviour across the compositions.

Completed with Dua under the supervision of Dr. Abdul Majeed Khan, Department of Physics, The Islamia University of Bahawalpur.
